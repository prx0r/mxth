# Rasa / QRI hypothesis layer
Do not optimize a universal `beauty` number.

Behavioral outcomes: liking, dwell/absorption, replay, return.
Structural descriptors: approximate symmetry, symmetry change, entropy, occupancy, edge/coherence,
later Laplacian eigenmode concentration, recurrence and compression progress.

Abhinavagupta supplies conceptual distinctions about aesthetic savor/commonization/absorption; QRI/STV
supplies testable but speculative structural hypotheses. Neither licenses the claim that a visual metric
*is* rasa or valence. Treat each relation as a preregistered empirical hypothesis.
