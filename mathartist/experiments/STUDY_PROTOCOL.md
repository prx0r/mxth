# Attention-v1 controlled study protocol

`STUDY` is the first-party randomized-assignment view. It deliberately ignores phenotype fitness when
assigning specimens. Assignments are balanced across frozen source variants and persisted per anonymous
session. Repeated visits avoid previously assigned specimens where possible.

Primary outcomes: 5-second survival, conditional 10-second survival, conditional 20-second survival.
Secondary: dwell, fullscreen, explicit favorite, replay. Structural descriptors are recorded separately.

The study is exploratory until a hypothesis/version is preregistered and frozen. Do not call any outcome
"rasa", "valence" or "beauty"; those are theoretical constructs/hypotheses, not direct measurements.
YouTube is an external observational replication environment and must remain analytically separate.
