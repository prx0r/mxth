# V3.3 — Data-first emergence protocol

## Core experiment
For each frozen seed program M and admitted parameter surface Θ:

1. Uniform/Sobol/randomly sample Θ at high volume.
2. Execute M(θ) with the minimal native observer.
3. Reject only technical failures (NaN, blank, crashed, irrecoverably clipped).
4. Archive all viable genotypes and lineage metadata.
5. Use morphology embeddings/descriptors only to *cover* phenotype space, never to predict beauty.
6. Present a diverse blind batch to the human.
7. POTENTIAL creates a local branch around θ*. It does not alter the global OPEN objective.
8. Preserve dead/ugly stepping stones because fertility may appear later.

## Human-subject layer
Record view/replay/POTENTIAL/branch and optional retrospective beauty, valence, absorption and rasa response. Do not expose QRI/rasa descriptors during selection. Structural analyses happen downstream and must be discovery/confirmation separated.

## V3.3 acceptance benchmark
- >=25 real attributed #つぶやきProcessing seeds fetched locally.
- >=1,000 blind descendants per seed (>=25k specimens).
- exact default sketch reproduced before mutation.
- 100% seed records have artist + original URL + source hash.
- OPEN selection invariant to injected favorite/dwell/beauty labels.
- report per-seed viable rate, morphology coverage, local sensitivity and descendant fertility.
- contact sheets are inspection artifacts, not optimization targets.

## Frontier-paper bridge
A paper/repo becomes a seed only after executable reproduction. Paper2Agent may assist extraction. The adapter must emit: immutable mechanism M, admitted Θ with evidence, minimal observer O, validation test. If no defensible Θ exists, do not force the paper into MathArtist.
